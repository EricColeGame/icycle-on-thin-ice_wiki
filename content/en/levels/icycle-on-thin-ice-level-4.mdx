## Frequently Asked Questions About Icycle On Thin Ice Level 4

### What is the secret to collecting all ice cubes in icycle on thin ice level 4?
To gather every ice cube without plummeting into hazards, unlock at least the tier-two vacuum upgrade before attempting a full completion run. ### How do I stop Dennis from crashing after big vertical drops?
Crashing upon landing usually happens because Dennis is tilted too far forward or backward when making contact with uneven surfaces. While airborne, tap your directional inputs gently to ensure both bicycle wheels touch the ground at the exact same horizontal plane. Deploying your umbrella right before impact also bleeds off downward velocity, ensuring a gentle touchdown.

### Why do platforms collapse before I even reach them?
Certain structural elements are connected to dynamic physics triggers. If you cycle forward at full speed, Dennis hits invisible proximity lines that release rolling boulders or collapsing vehicles early. In these segments, approach ledges at a moderate pace to trigger the hazard, let the debris clear the path, and then jump across the resulting gap.
