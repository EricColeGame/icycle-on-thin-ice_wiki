## Step-by-Step Walkthrough: Conquering the First Route

Starting **icycle on thin ice level 1**, Dennis drops onto an icy incline. Resist the urge to hold full throttle immediately. The track is built to teach you spatial awareness and reaction timing. 

### Phase 1: The Initial Descent and First Gap
As the level opens, Dennis rolls downward along a slick ice slope. Gather speed naturally without tapping the reverse key. Just ahead, the terrain drops off into a small chasm lined with razor-sharp icicles. 

Hit your jump button roughly one bicycle-length before the ledge edge. You will sail cleanly over the hazard. If you misjudge the distance, tap and hold your jump to deploy your starter umbrella for a minor glide buffer.

### Phase 2: Collapsing Bridges and Overhead Hazards
After clearing the first gap, you encounter floating platforms that tilt and crack under Dennis's weight. Speed is essential here. If you linger on these unstable sheets of ice, they give way, sending Dennis plummeting into the abyss. Keep your forward momentum steady. 

Watch the ceiling as you ride past the middle checkpoint. Overhead icicles dislodge when you cross specific invisible triggers. According to community reports and speedrunning tests, tapping reverse for a split second right as you see the ceiling shake lets the spikes drop harmlessly ahead of you before you hop over their shattered remains.

### Phase 3: The Final Leap to the Exit
The final stretch of the level features a steep downward curve leading toward an expansive chasm. You need maximum momentum here. Do not pump the brakes; allow gravity to do the work. Leap at the crest of the slope, open your umbrella mid-air, and drift smoothly toward the goal marker.

To grab the low cubes beneath the first major drop, do not make a high-speed leap. Instead, roll slowly off the edge, deploy your umbrella immediately, and drift backward slightly into the alcove.

### What is the reward for completing all Level 1 challenges?

### Does Dennis move faster if I continuously tap the pedal key?
No. Holding the forward key provides consistent acceleration up to Dennis's top cycling speed. Pumping or spamming the directional button actually disrupts your momentum on slick ice surfaces.
