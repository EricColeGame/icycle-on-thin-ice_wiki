1. Save up your gathered ice cubes to unlock the retro arcade terminal in the menu shop.

## Frequently Asked Questions About iCycle on Thin Ice Achievements

### Which of the iCycle on Thin Ice achievements is the hardest to complete?
Most players find **"Get 100% in all dreams from the scrapbook"** to be the most demanding. It requires clearing all 60 challenge stars across the 20 locations, unlocking every wardrobe cosmetic, discovering all five secret chambers, and beating the classic retro campaign.

### Do deaths reset my progress when farming ice cubes or candy explosions?
No. Lifetime milestone statistics—such as exploding 100 bombs or collecting 1,000 ice cubes—save to your profile immediately upon interaction. If you detonate a cherry bomb or grab a cluster of cubes and immediately die or restart the checkpoint, those actions still count toward your total achievements tally.

### How do I unlock the original Icycle 1 retro stages?
The retro *Icycle 1* stages are unlocked via the in-game shop. Collect ice cubes during your main campaign runs, visit the store menu, and purchase the classic cabinet unlock. Once purchased, you can play through all 8 original web-era levels directly from the bonus menu to unlock the retro achievement.

### Can I complete the Location 1–20 challenge achievements in any order?
Yes. You do not need to earn all three challenge stars on a stage before advancing the story. In fact, returning to earlier locations after purchasing advanced items—like the extended umbrella glider and the ice cube vacuum—makes completing the speedrun, collection, and survival challenges significantly easier.
