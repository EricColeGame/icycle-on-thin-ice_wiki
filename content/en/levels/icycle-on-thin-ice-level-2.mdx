### How do I collect all ice cubes in icycle on thin ice level 2?
To collect every single cube, it is best to unlock the Ice Vacuum upgrade from the Icebox shop before attempting a completionist run. ### Can I beat level 2 without equipping an umbrella?
Yes, level 2 is fully beatable without deploying an umbrella, and doing so satisfies a specific in-game mission objective. You must maximize your downhill wheel speed on every incline and jump at the very lip of each ramp to gain enough arc and distance to bridge the gaps without floating.

### Why does Dennis keep falling backward on platform landings?
Dennis's balance shifts depending on directional inputs while airborne. If you hold the forward key the entire time you are in the air, Dennis may pitch forward and faceplant, or rebound backward upon touching uneven snow. Release directional inputs just before touchdown to land squarely on both wheels.

### What is the most important Icebox upgrade for early levels?
The basic umbrella and the first level of the Ice Vacuum are by far the most impactful investments. The umbrella provides a safety net during long falls, while the vacuum dramatically increases your cube collection rate, accelerating your ability to purchase further perks and life extensions.
