## Stage-by-Stage Strategies for Dennis's Dreams

Conquering the **icycle on thin ice bonus levels** requires distinct tactical adjustments compared to ordinary levels. Trolley navigation hinges on managing downhill momentum, anticipating sudden track collapses, and conserving glide stamina.

### Dream 1: Slumbering Cart
The introductory dream eases players into shopping cart handling. Use the gentle ramps to practice jumping just before the apex of each slope. Keep your umbrella closed during flat stretches to maintain forward speed, opening it only when clearing broad tea-saucer gaps.

### Dream 2: Velvet Corridor
This stage introduces spring-loaded surfaces modeled after vintage furniture cushions. Bouncing off these points launches Dennis into towering vertical trajectories. Hold your vacuum active at the peak of each launch to vacuum up clusters of shards without deviating from your safe landing path.

### Dream 3: Humbug Carnival
Featuring exploding candy hazards inspired by heavy acoustic foley effects, this level punishes hesitation. Timed detonations launch Dennis across broad expanses. Wait for the humbug's telltale pulse before rolling into range, letting the shockwave boost your cart over otherwise fatal spikes.

### Dream 4: Subconscious Abyss
Visual cues become deliberately inverted and confusing in this monochrome nightmare. Rely on audio signals—such as the creaking pitch-shifted tape effects indicating falling structures—to time your jumps. Keep your umbrella open for micro-adjustments whenever falling through dense debris fields.

### Dream 5: The Wedding Waltz
The ultimate dream sequence demands flawless muscle memory. Dennis hurtles down a rapidly disintegrating multi-tiered slope while giant hazards tumble overhead. Prioritize survival over immediate item collection on your first run; you can replay the stage with top-tier vacuum upgrades later to pull in missed collectibles cleanly.
