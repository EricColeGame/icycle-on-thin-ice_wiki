## Complete Walkthrough: Conquering the 5 Dream Levels

Every sequence in the dream catalogue presents unique physics. When traversing the **icycle on thin ice dreams**, standard cycling inertia is often modified by low friction, low gravity, or sudden bursts of reverse momentum.

### Dream 1: The Shopping Cart Odyssey

Dennis swaps his iconic bicycle for a squeaky shopping cart. The cart carries significantly more forward momentum than the bicycle, which means braking requires early input.

1. **The Initial Descent**: Drop down the opening slope without holding forward acceleration. Let gravity establish momentum.
2. **Clearing the Velvet Couches**: Jump when the front wheel reaches the edge of the first platform; deploy the umbrella halfway through the arc.
3. **The Mirror Maze Transition**: As shifting picture frames drift into view, stay in the center channel to avoid getting crushed between collapsing margins.

### Dream 2: Domestic Nightmare

This stage simulates the chaotic breakdown of domestic life. Teapots, refrigerators, and dining chairs plummet from the pastel-colored sky.

- **Pacing**: Maintain a slow, deliberate cadence. Charging forward causes Dennis to run into debris before sound cues give adequate warning.
- **Auditory Cues**: Listen for falling sound effects. When you hear the telltale clatter of metal plates, pull Dennis back or hover under an existing overhang.
- **Shard Cache**: A cluster of 15 shards sits underneath the floating dining table. Use a high-tier vacuum cleaner to secure them without landing on the disintegrating lower rug.

### Dream 3: Candy-Coated Madness

Surreal giant humbugs and confections litter the floor. In this dream, certain floor hazards detonate upon contact, launching Dennis across the screen.

### Dream 4: The Twisted Funfair

One of the hardest bonus sequences in the game, Dream 4 places Dennis on track loops that mimic a runaway rollercoaster. Centrifugal force holds Dennis onto curved loops, but mistimed jumps launch him into the abyss.

- **Loop Dynamics**: Never jump while inverted on an internal loop. Wait until your wheels transition to a level surface before initiating a jump.
- **Rail Transitions**: When tracks split, steer toward upper rails to snag hidden ice crystals, as lower tracks are lined with spike traps.

### Dream 5: The Subconscious Void

The final dream represents Dennis confronting his fate. Architectural structures shatter into geometric fragments. Platforms do not respawn once they crumble beneath Dennis's wheels.

1. Equip your highest-tier umbrella before launching this stage.
2. Rely heavily on glide feathering—tap the glide button repeatedly rather than holding it down to extend horizontal distance.
3. Aim directly for central floating silhouettes; peripheral paths contain bonus shards but carry an extremely high mortality rate.
