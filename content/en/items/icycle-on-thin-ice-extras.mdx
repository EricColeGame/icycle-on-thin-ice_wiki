## Frequently Asked Questions About Icycle on Thin Ice Extras

### What is the hardest of the icycle on thin ice extras to unlock?
Player experience consistently points to the original retro *Icycle* game as the most demanding unlockable. Unlocking it requires finishing all 80 in-game mission objectives, many of which demand pixel-perfect bicycle handling and zero-death runs across unforgiving levels.

### Do icycle on thin ice extras affect gameplay performance or physics?
Functional gear upgrades—specifically umbrellas and vacuum cleaners—dramatically impact traversal physics, fall speed, and collection hitboxes. However, cosmetic **icycle on thin ice extras**, such as Dennis’s hats, trousers, and novelty outfits, provide purely visual customization and do not grant armor or speed boosts.

### How do you unlock the 5 bonus Dream Levels?
The bonus Dream Levels unlock progressively by accumulating specific ice shard milestones and clearing mission sets across the primary campaign chapters. Unlike standard stages, these surreal levels place Dennis in unique vehicles like shopping carts and feature dreamlike platforming puzzles.

### Can you replay Dream Levels once they are unlocked?
Yes. Once unlocked, all bonus dream levels and the classic retro game can be selected and replayed at any time directly from the level select menu, allowing you to optimize your high scores and collect missed shards.
